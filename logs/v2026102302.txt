## 性能结果
同一 V100、真实 LIME `8×8×8×16` 前缀、c64、2 levels、`2^4` block、`nvec=12`、tol `1e-6`：

| 路径 | steady median |
|---|---:|
| CUDA Strict | 0.021693 s |
| QUDA aligned MG | 0.144755 s |
| CUDA Legacy initial-deflation | 0.009302 s |
| CUDA Legacy fine-only | 0.008500 s |
| Torch Legacy | 0.607062 s |
| Torch Strict | 1.670798 s |
| KUPL Legacy | 0.256530 s |
| KUPL Strict | 0.268440 s |
