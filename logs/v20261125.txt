Wheel behaviour, measured while checking that no foreign library can ship
  Building a wheel from the sdist compiles what the local toolchain supports: with
  nvcc present it produced pyqcu/cuda/libqcu.so (28,139,952 B) + the Cython bridge +
  pyqcu/kupl/libqcu.so and was tagged cp312-cp312-linux_x86_64; with NVCC pointing at
  a nonexistent path the CUDA library disappears and the wheel shrinks from 17.7 MB to
  1.37 MB.  Nothing is copied from the worktree.  This is also the demonstration that
  the 28 MB library seen in an installed tree is built, not smuggled.
  Known non-blocking: because ext_modules is unconditionally non-empty for the default
  backend, a library-less wheel is still tagged cp312-cp312-linux_x86_64.  Over-tagged,
  never leaky; fix scheduled for 0.1.20.  Wheel is not published either way - only
  sdist - because libqcu.so is not portable.

What shipped, in one paragraph
  The KUPL Legacy-MG is now the CUDA algorithm rather than an approximation of it:
  right-preconditioned FGMRES(10) whose preconditioner is nu_pre MR smoothing steps,
  one coarse cycle, and a prolongation added with weight 1, with no post-smoothing and
  CUDA's contraction gate (window 6, five ratios over six, threshold 0.9 per window =
  0.8812 per step).  The old BiCGStab outer loop, the correction-interval scheduling,
  the analytic optimal damping, the Krylov "prefix" smoothing and the fallback-to-fine
  path are deleted, not switched off.  Two genuine defects were found and fixed while
  porting: the preconditioner zeroed levels_[0].x, which IS the outer solver's solution
  vector, so every accumulated update was discarded (true residual rose 0.497 -> 5.741
  across 21 restarts while the Givens estimate sat at 1e-3); and the complex Givens
  rotation was not unitary (c = conj(a)/r is unitary only for real a; the second row
  was force-written to zero, hiding it).  The algorithm now halves the iteration count
  (28 -> 14) and is still 1.41x slower, because one preconditioner application costs
  2.82x a plain apply and 2.82 > 2.  That is the archived result, measured with job
  numbers, not an estimate: KUPL MG does not accelerate on this hardware, and the
  cause is structural rather than a tuning miss - a five-arm sweep over nu_pre and the
  coarse tolerance moved wall clock by 3.7%.
